The kind of dark that echoes, that seeps. It soaks into your skin and drips from the white cloth swaying in the breeze. You see your hand at your side and you reach for it, dragging yourself across the wet cobblestone. You're cold, you're so cold. You see blood and bone, the grotesquery of betrayal. You see a body flayed, a man with no head, the gold ink of promise. Faster and faster it spins round and round. A castle, a lake, the sun, the ink, the gold, the promise, your promise, his promise. You're spinning now, spinning with the headless man. The two of you twist round in a waltz with no name and the headless man looks at you with no eyes and grips your shoulder and you repeat your vow, your life tied to it. What was it? What did you promise him? The headless man smiles without lips and leaves you desolate. "No one here is going to hurt you... I promise." You stand before a sea of skin, an abandoned coat of a creature with no need of it. The pink flesh rolls in gentle waves, rustling with the sound of palm against palm. As you look into the waves you feel a decision has been made. The scales have be weighed. And you're thrown awake.
