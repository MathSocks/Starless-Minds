Gods are spawned through significant events. If a mortal event occurs with enough emotion, a God will be spawned out of the concept being practiced to the extreme.

There are three tiers of gods:

>[!infobox]
> <u> Examples of Primordial Gods </u>
> - [[Malar]], God of hunger and the hunt
> - [[Mystra]], Goddess of magic
#### Primordial
Primordial gods are old gods that represent key life experiences and aspects, such as life, death and hunger

---


>[!infobox]
> Examples of Senescent Gods
> - [[Torm]], God of courage and sacrifice
#### Senescent
Senescent gods are gods that represent widely experienced aspects of life, but that aren't key to living. Examples are courage and sacrifice.

---

>[!infobox]
> Examples of Embryonic Gods
> - [[Zac]], Slayer of Kings and Mortal Genitalia

#### Embryonic
Embryonic gods are new gods who are not widely known or popular. They embody aspects that are not widely experienced by people, such as slaying kings.
