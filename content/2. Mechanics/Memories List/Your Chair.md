### The Memory Overview

You stand in the doorway facing out towards a porch, watching the last light of the sun melt into the endless sky like blood in an ink pot. There's someone behind you , talking incessantly as you thump your boots together, spraying dried mud onto the porch. Despite your rhythmic thumping the mud clings persistently, thick, heavy. You'd have better luck taking a knife to the boot and carving away at the layers, so you dump them in the doorway. You wander past the person chattering in your ear and sink into an armchair in front of the fireplace. Your chair but not your chair. Your bones ache. They're heavy. So heavy. Heavy with life. Heavy with death. Heavy with hate.
