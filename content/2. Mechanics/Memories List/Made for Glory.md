### The Memory Overview

Someone takes your hand firmly, like they're worried you'll slip away into nothing. A hood obscures their eyes but they smile at you reassuringly, leading you up a wide, ornate staircase. From behind you whispers slither up the steps, peering over your shoulder and watching you with interest. You know that you are about to be greater than you have ever been. It's not a good feeling. Your too small to be great, and although your mind is young you already understand that your tiny hands, though they must, cannot hold greatness. It will spill out between your thumbs, through the gaps in your fleshy little fingers and into every facet of your life.
