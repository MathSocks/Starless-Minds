### The Memory Overview

You don't scrawl. These aren't the writings of a madman. Some desperate loon. Your hand is steady as hangs over the letter like a body from a noose, over a name marked in ink. You weigh your choice for the final time, the wheels you'll set in motion. You have the power, you have the connections. All you have to do, is send that little letter. With his hands, man carved his own rivers, built his dams. Not just because he could, but because for all nature's grandure, he knew better. That is what your father told you. You seal the letter. You carve the course.
