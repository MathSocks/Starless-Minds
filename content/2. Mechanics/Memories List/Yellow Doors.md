### The Memory Overview

You round the corner together and he holds your hand like you might wander off. When he comes to a hault in the hall you bump into his back and peer over his shoulder like a soldier peering from a trench. She stands by the door like a little warden, arms crossed and lips tight. You sit on your haunches as they argue, never louder than a whisper, staring at the yellow door. You debate crawling past them and slipping inside, just to feel that warmth for a few minutes, even if you got in trouble. The thought dies quickly, one of them would catch you, you're still too slow. The two come to an agreement you don't really hear before he takes your hand again and starts to drag you away. You watch the yellow door dissapear back down the long hall.
