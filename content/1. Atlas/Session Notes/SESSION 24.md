### Session Summary:

Party enters into Daffodints quarters, and see Elel leaning against the wall. As sean and Folssa walk in and Elel eyes them off, Sean makes a “he’s checking us out” comment, to which folssa laughs. Elel goes mhm sure thats definitely what I am doing. He points the party into a room where daffodint waits. The party sits opposite elel at the table with Daffodint at the end. Daffodint welcomes the party

To get through to level 12 we need to go through level 4, as the rooms of torm rotate around. One person must distract Bebo while others go through with a guidestone. Under no circumstances can Bebo see the stone and know you’re cheating.

The plan is we need to destroy the imprisonment spell that is trapping torm, and place a jar in that room to collect something that will be sent back to daffodin. It is maintained by multiple wizards who are casting the spell over and over. Taking out 4-5 wizards should drop the spell. We have six guidestone charges total, spread between two stones.

Elel can get us to a designated entrance. Someone will cast invisibility on Folssa, who will book it and scout the area. The group will regroup, discuss the plan, and go from there. The current working idea is that odessa and ghesh can cast silence to take out wizards, and we all book it to the planned escape. When the group has finished planning, Daffodin guides them to an armoury, where they get resources to help with their mission.

He starts moving on all fours towards us. Nope fuck this shit, Folssa throws the hat to the far end of the hall, and bebo snaps towards it and gallops towards it. The rest of the group book it.

When in the hidden platform area of level 12, Divario casts invisibility on Folssa, and Folssa climbs up and starts scouting around the area. In the area they arrive in, they all can feel the suffocating pressure of the energy of torm, much more than previous levels.

In the entrance to the main area level 12, the aura is almost suffocating. Being this close, it overwhelms you. Folssa fights nausea as he glances upon the orb containing torm and his energy over the last 3 months. DO NOT PONDER THE ORB.

to the right, big fountain room, another fuck you tower. Through hallway, fuck you tower facing them. the hidden rooms have no doorways, if you can’t get in there normally, kma probably can’t either.

hallway around the back is normal hallway. wall is thick, but very explodable.

Army room has about 75 people. massive machine thing protected at far end. There’s soldier tents, head guy tent and campfires.

Folssa heads back, goes to climb down but slips and accidentally yells. Soldiers come to scope, divario covers everyone with the invisibility and hides behind a statue. The soldiers go in, sweeping their definitely-not-guns around. It swipes past Faolan’s nose, above folssa’s hat, but no one is found.

Odessa casts invisibility on divario. The group proceed towards the main room, the overwhelming torm magic challenges divario and odessa’s concentration. odessa pulls through, divario wavers but he pulls through - but the invisibility drops on folssa. All four watchtowers point at him. Odessa pulls folssa under her spell, but its too late. Folssa messages the rest of the team - do not break your concentration, i will give myself up, they don’t know you’re here. Go go go. He drops a lightning javelin in the hopes someone can pick it up, and ghesh stuffs the bag of holding quickly behind him, and folssa sticks it up the back of his shirt.  He is no longer a willing participant. As soon as he’s out of the spell, the four towers circle in on him. Bells ring out, and ten soldiers surround folssa. They yell at him where did he come from, folssa goes I climbed i climbed i swear, arrow to knee, he is dragged away and party hears him scream

Divario has to fight to maintain concentration, but the party books it to the fountain room and through. Past narrow hallway, patrol is running around looking for possible other people in tower. Sean trips, is kicked, guy goes I GOT ONE. Ghesh pulls sean backwards as sean uses bag of tricks and pulls out a massive fucking great elk. The patrol think its a druid and shoot it. Party gets by, up and heads to planned escape. Ghesh casts detect magic on the area and immediately regrets it, as torm magic overwhelms her and her nose starts bleeding. odessa shoves her sleeve in Ghesh’s face to stem the bleeding. Sean moulds earth, creates a hole and Elel and divario go inside. Divario maintains concentration on everyone else so they stay invisible, elel sets up guidestone.

Folssa is tied up in the army room. They have taken all of his weapons and resources (fuckers), but luckily did not find the bag of holding. Folssa is bound at his wrists and his ankles to a chair. A commander guy enters the room, and starts interrogating folssa. He asks where he came from and how he got there, Folssa continues to maintain he climbed the tower. The commander goes I know you didn’t climb the tower, because you would have had to go through level 10, which is controlled by my men, and level 11, also controlled by my men. How did you get here. “I fucking climbed”. He slaps folssa across the face. Commander says we found your druid in the far hallway, and my men took care of them. Folssa’s first thought is shit sean is dead, i am a dead man walking, but he maintains a straight face at the commander.
