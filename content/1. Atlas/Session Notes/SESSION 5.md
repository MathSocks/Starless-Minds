### Session Summary:

The party spends the day preparing for the battle with [[Odessa Velle]] at the [[Abandoned Gallows]]. [[Ghesh]] and [[Bodie Stomart]] check out the library and jewellers, then head to the gallows. [[Divario]] and [[Folssa]] successfully interrogate the [[Egglord]] and break in to his hideout, where they secure pipebombs and meet at the gallows. [[Faolan]] heads into the woods to hunt, but is plagued by hunger and his impending curse. [[Sean]] heads to the markets and purchases a series of trinkets, including [[The Purple Pot]]. [[Ghesh]], [[Divario]] and [[Folssa]] engage in battle with [[Odessa Velle]] in the [[Gallow's Arena]], but are drawn upwards to the town centre where a massive werewolf ([[Faolan]]) scales the town wall and kills a townsperson. The ensuing battle results in the town's destruction, and the "death" of the werewolf, revealing [[Faolan]] beneath. The party was chased from [[Westtown]] through the [[Gallow's Arena]], and through a long passageway found themselves in the fields of [[South Edren]]. 

Throughout the entirety of the battle and the town's destruction, [[Sean]] could not be found, for he was indulging in a long and probably very enjoyable drug trip.

#### Divario goes to potion stall
At the potion store, [[Divario]] is greeted by a very dodgy looking shopkeeper with a heavy smokers cough. He buys four health [[Potions]] for 50 silver each. 

On his way back, [[Divario]] contacts [[Zac]], who remembers that [[Divario]] is supposed to be doing tasks for him, and he asks [[Divario]] if he can kill a cool monster. [[Divario]] agrees. He then travels to the gallows to reunite with the rest of the party. 

#### Folssa, Ghesh, Divario and Bodie explore the gallows
[[Bodie Stomart]] reveals that the battle site is located underneath the gallows. [[Folssa]] punches out the side of the gallows, revealing winding stairs down underground. 

[[Divario]] and [[Bodie Stomart]] proceed to ungracefully lose their footing and tumble down the stairs in a tangle of arms and legs, and land in the water of the [[Gallow's Arena]] below. There is no trace of magic in the place, except for a part of the waterbed in a small alcove. [[Folssa]] uncovers the [[Wind Jar]] buried in the waterbed, which is passed to [[Bodie Stomart]].

To [[Folssa]], the arena seems nostalgic, like a soft, pleasant childhood memory.

#### Battle preparations
[[Bodie Stomart]] goes off to hide in one of the side passages. [[Divario]] hides himself and the three pipebombs on top of a broken pillar. [[Ghesh]] and [[Folssa]] aim to lure [[Odessa Velle]] as close to [[Divario]] as possible, so that [[Divario]] can throw the pipebombs and cast burning hands to set them off at [[Odessa Velle]].

#### The Battle
[[Odessa Velle]] appears at the top of the staircase. [[Folssa]] and [[Odessa Velle]] grapple for a bit until [[Folssa]] manages to bring [[Odessa Velle]] to the base of the stairs. [[Ghesh]] and [[Folssa]] run for cover as [[Divario]] throws two of the three pipebombs at [[Odessa Velle]], the third exploding behind him as he leaps from the broken pillar.

[[Odessa Velle]] is sent flying backwards from the explosion into a wall, which caves in to reveal another section of the arena. [[Odessa Velle]] escapes up the staircases in the new section and uses her magic to create two other illusions of herself. [[Divario]] makes himself look like [[Bodie Stomart]] and deceives [[Odessa Velle]] by shouting that [[Ghesh]] was caught in the bomb crossfire and hurt. Out of concern for [[Ghesh]], [[Odessa Velle]]'s spell dissipates and she rushes to the main atrium, where [[Divario]] (disguised as [[Bodie Stomart]]) casts burning hands in her face.

A pissed [[Odessa Velle]] runs back into the higher section of the arena, where a pillar in the middle of the room begins to rise. [[Ghesh]] and [[Bodie Stomart]] climb their way onto the pillar with [[Odessa Velle]]. The fight becomes split between [[The Chucklefuckers]] and [[Odessa Velle]] wanting to finish the battle, and [[Bodie Stomart]] and [[Ghesh]] fighting to stop it. Eventually the pillar rises up into the town centre, and [[Divario]] and [[Folssa]] run back through the [[Gallow's Arena]] to find [[Odessa Velle]], [[Bodie Stomart]] and [[Ghesh]] in the centre. [[Odessa Velle]]'s final act of the battle is to set the [[Dwarf Statues]] alight with fire bolt.

#### The Werewolf Fight
A large werewolf scales the town wall and kills the nearest villager. The party + [[Odessa Velle]] begin to fight the werewolf. The werewolf had immunities to all of [[Folssa]]'s attacks, so [[Folssa]] runs to the markets to gather supplies. [[Divario]] and [[Odessa Velle]] team up to attack the werewolf from a distance, while [[Ghesh]] attacked from closer in. [[Folssa]] returns with a bunch of healing and buff [[Potions]], and then runs to the [[Westtown Blacksmith]] in search of weaponry. [[Divario]] and [[Odessa Velle]] fire at the nearby windmills, which collapses onto the werewolf. [[Odessa Velle]] then casts a spell to make the werewolf levitate and become immobile. [[Folssa]] then returns with the [[Silver Morningstar]]. The werewolf casts a spell which [[Folssa]] dodges but kills [[Ghesh]]. [[Folssa]] then hits the werewolf with the [[Silver Morningstar]], and [[Odessa Velle]] finishes off the werewolf with firebolt.

#### Reunions
The skin of the defeated werewolf seems to fall into itself to reveal [[Faolan]]. [[Divario]] rushes to [[Faolan]], while [[Folssa]] follows behind and offers a [[Health Potion]]. [[Ghesh]] is crowded by [[Odessa Velle]] and [[Bodie Stomart]] and also receives a [[Health Potion]]. [[Odessa Velle]] gives [[Ghesh]] a forehead kiss. The guards of the town surround the party and demand to put their hands up and give up [[Faolan]]. The party flees the scene by running to the [[Gallow's Arena]]. They take a moment as [[Faolan]] wakes up and immediately starts panicking. [[Divario]] tries to comfort, and asks "Did it hurt?". The party turns down a side passage which leads to [[South Edren]].

Passage leads to large field. [[Ghesh]] curls up with [[Bodie Stomart]], with [[Odessa Velle]] nearby. [[Divario]] keeps an eye on [[Faolan]]. [[Folssa]] offers the group cigarettes, which [[Odessa Velle]] and [[Divario]] accept. The group takes a break before moving onwards.
