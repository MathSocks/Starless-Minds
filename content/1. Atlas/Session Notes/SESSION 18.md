### Timeline of Events
Ghesh experiences [[Curse of Hyder]] dream.

The party wakes up in a weird castle hall, lots of mirrors, clothing racks with jackets and hats, paintings etc, but the hallway is completely empty. There’s the clanging of cutlery and plates in another room. Divario looks down the hallway - the longer he stares, the more it seems to twist and warp, like you’ve had too many drinks. When you look away and break eye contact, it goes away.
Folssa has cleared torm sickness, Faolan is now sick with stage 1. Ghesh is busy writing panicked into her journal. 

The paintings in the hallway are paintings of lord and ladies, high born people - most humans, but a decent mix of other races too. They’re all of elaborate sets and whatnot. One painting stands out - a long portrait of lords and ladies at a feasting table. Hunting dogs under the table. Lots of plants and food being passed around. The host and lady are in the centre. The lady has long auburn hair - the lord has no face. It looks like his face has been sliced off, and you can see the inner tissue, the cavity of his mouth, the back of his eyes, and the back of his brain. No one in the painting seems to notice. Ghesh casts detect undead - its just a painting. Divario remembers that torm had a statue with no face, which mimics the damage on the painting face. Folssa can see the lady has her hands folded in her lap, long auburn hair with a plesantly smiling face, and she leans into her lord husband. Odessa suggests someone painted over top to defile the painting - the techniques of the painting have really textured layers so its hard to identify if someone painted over. Divario leans towards no.

The party goes down the hall - it starts twisting and turning until everyone enters the light, and it snaps back to a large dining hall. The hall is filled with large tables, skeletons seated around. Their clothes, flesh and hair has rotten away, leaving only their bones and rusted jewellery. They seem to ‘talk’ to each other, trying to consume food that falls into their ribcages. Down the end of the hall, ghesh can see a tall entertainment person that looks like a jester, and someone else that looks alive in a corner.

The valif brothers hang back while the party tries to sneak into the hall and hide from the jester. Ghesh, Folssa and Divario wriggle under a table by the hunter guy. Faolan, Sean, Constance and Odessa get caught by the sight of the Jester. He was standing and juggling, but his head whips around to see everyone, and he cartwheels down the hall to the others. His limbs seem to stretch like rubber hose. As he back handsprings and lands in front of the group, he yells “New people!!!”. As he looks around, his neck seems to extend to peer at everyone. He moves with cartoon logic. Sean asks the guy whats his name - he says Bebo. Bebo asks are the group here for Bebo, Sean says yes, and bebo is so happy. He says everyone wants to go to the next level and they miss out. His first trick - he starts juggling, spits out his heart and starts juggling that as well, before his tongue sticking out like rubber and swallowing it back again. Bebo has a menace to him when Faolan is the only one not clapping at him.

Divario, Folssa and Ghesh are hiding under the table. They make it over to the feet of the other guy. Divario pulls on the guy’s cloak, the guy doesn’t seem impressed. Divario asks whats going on, the guy says he’s just here for the free chicken. Folssa asks why is the jester ignoring him, the guy says he’s already been through to the next level, he’s just back for the chicken. Ghesh asks how to get to the next level, the guy says you gotta ask to eat with the jester and do his challenge. Ghesh does an arcana check on him - he absolutely reeks of torm magic - ghesh also notices the hunter guy absolutely reeks of it too. Ghesh asks the guy how long he has been in torm, the guy says he can’t remember. Ghesh asks about the NorthBrook guys, the guy calls the people who live there Roaches - they live in the cracks and don’t die.

The jester says “hold your applause”, and flicks his arm, doing a wave motion and rolling his arm up snail shell until it reaches his head, his head and neck join in to the other end of his arms, and it all slingshots back. Faolan claps, the jester is overjoyed.
Ghesh messages everyone to ask to eat with him. Sean tries to subtly ask him to eat - he must be hungry - the Jester says oh you’re wanting to play my favourite game, you want to leave already??? Sean tries to convince him noooo, we just want to play with you. He’s successful, and Bebo grasps his hands saying “no one likes Bebo’s tricks but you”. Sean says I have a trick for you, claps his hands, and the other three party members under the table jump out in a poof of smoke and fireworks. Divario sucessfully convinces Bebo that everyone knows of bebo from all the lands.
Game rules: group of five, everyone sits down and gets meals. One meal is “really yucky”, but you don’t know which is the bad one. If you eat a tasty meal, you can leave. “But you won’t leave if you eat a tasty meal, right?” Sean says he wouldn’t dream of it. Sean asks what happens if you eat a yucky meal, jester says you gotta go again. Sean asks if that is how the skeletons are here, bebo says noooo they’re Hemlock's buddies, the bastard. Sean initially tries to make Bebo make a promise, and Bebo says nooo oaths are for knights, and knights are as loyal to a promise as a king to a whore.

Sean, Constance, Divario and Faolan join Bebo at a table to eat. Sean tries to secretly cast detect poison and disease - there’s no difference between any of them. Divario does the sniff test, it smells like food. Faolan does the same. Everyone takes a bite. Faolan swallows, and as soon as the food hits your throat, it switches taste, swells and tries to choke Faolan. From their mouth, maggots spill out, and Faolan begins suffocating. Bebo is laughing and clapping joyfully. Divario grabs Faolan and starts Heimlick manoeuvring Faolan. Hundreds of maggots just spill - they keep multiplying constantly. Folssa shoves a healing potion (17 left) down Faolan’s throat, but the maggots are so thick it doesn’t go down. As faolan’s vision blurs, the white hand pushes Faolan’s hand to the wine glass in the centre of the table. The wine is acid, but it clears the maggots from their throat, and Faolan throws up bile. Sean has a verbal go at Bebo, Bebo says this is what you signed up for, this is the game and I wasn’t lying that it was yucky. Divario and Ghesh end up in a shouting match with Divario trying to convince ghesh to join an affinity. Ghesh is trying to convince him no, if we kill the guy we’re stuck. Ghesh eventually manages to successfully back him down. Sean argues with Bebo a bit more, Bebo ends up saying that in order to finish the game, only four people can leave the table - aka someone needs to die. Odessa manages to convince bebo that the group wants to step away for a moment to talk about how good the game is. Constance takes Sean away and tries to convince him that he should pick his two least favourite people to die - minotaur brothers (FUCK YOU CONSTANCE).

After some scheming, the group decided to go one by one, with Folssa near to swipe the wine if needed. Divario eats, and the food goes down smoothly. Folssa books it with the wine and dashes 130ft away. Bebo goes after folssa as he runs, but begins to fail and choke on maggots. Divario leaps up and slams his Anderes blade into Bebo’s arm. Bebo twitches, and goes still. Everyone is wondering if he’ll get up, the hunter guy says yeahhh he’ll be back.

The group schemes a bit, Bebo comes back and Sean sucks up to him a bit. Sean goes one on one, he’s clear, Folssa books it again with the wine. Next round, ghesh, odessa and lootra - ghesh and odessa have rats. Ghesh’s rat gets sick, Ghesh quickly bites her food and sips the wine. Next round, Odessa is clear, passes the rat to Ghesh and it chokes. Folssa manages to distract Bebo long enough for ghesh to swap the plates. Bebo dies. Next round, Gart, Constance, Faolan, and Turtan. Folssa sticks cocaine in a skeleton guy’s ribcage to distract Bebo. Rat tasting - constance gets it (HA). Constance swaps to Gart, Gart swaps to bebo but Bebo suddenly decides lets swap everyones plates. A new round starts, folssa distracts by showing his fancy abs and cocaine doing cartwheels over his shoulders. All players are clear and bebo gets the poison.

Finally only folssa left. He just goes for it, bites the food and gets the maggots. Second round, folssa fine, he books it and bebo dies again. Fuck that guy. Folssa steals his hat, and also adds a bell to his hat.
