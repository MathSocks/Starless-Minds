### Timeline of Events
Bud and Folssa wait outside the seamstress shop - Nora’s Needle. The others are inside. First room is stacked full of fabrics and boxes and all sorts of stuff. There’s a magic needle and thread self weaving at the counter. The person leading the group in calls out for Ms Stormrock - a little dwarf woman with exaggerated needles sticking out of her bun. The person leading in goes oh we need a med kit. Stormrock goes oh yeah sure and scans the group, locking eyes on Sean and going oh is that you Sean??? How are you??? Sean recognises this lady as Lasvia, a friend of his mother’s, she doesn’t look like she’s aged a day. She specialises in disguises, especially disguise cloaks.

Sean awkwardly engages with her - she asks where he’s been, he looks so much like his siblings, etc etc. Mhm mhm *get me out of here*. Stormrock fixes everyone a cup of tea and invites folssa and bud back inside. Asks if everyone is a travelling group, yeah yeah a travelling group that works. Asks about “[folssa’s] uncle”, why does he have a metal helmet - oh uhh he’s odd, brain damage ya know, its brain protection. Bud goes I’m fashionable. Yes you are bud.

Stormrock says she hasn’t seen Sean around much during the day, does he have his nightcap? What’s a nightcap? Oh you must be new in town, you wear them if you gotta run errands late at night, just fills your lungs with something other than the bad air. I’ll grab one for each of you and your friends, and your animals. 

Ghesh asks about the disguise cloaks - they’d be 400 gold to get one for each member. Stormrock is like I can do that, what do you want to look like. Oh my god we get to pick!? Ghesh picks to look like three kobolds in a trenchcoat in drag again, Faolan is emo but not a furry anymore, Sean and bud are dressed as drag queens, and Folssa is a terrible goose in drag. Stormrock really likes the ideas - Oh sean your friends are so creative!

Stormrock asks the group do you have any questions? Do you know somewhere we could find a magical item to overload/clear the bird? Stormrock says oh there’s a lot around, as you go out further they become dodgier and dodgier. Sean says would the black market have them? Oh don’t go there, good thing you have your disguises. Ghesh asks about where to find Kurick, Stormrock goes if you’re after someone specific go find a whisperward. They’re in the place Sean said he WASN’T gonna go to (cough cough black markets), you’ll need to haggle them they’ll try scam you, but they’re efficient.

Thank you stormrock we leave. Time to go to the black markets! Ghesh goes wait a minute where’s the markets Sean? Mmmmmmmmm they may or may not be just outside the safe zone??? It’ll be fine man trust we have the nightcaps. Well can we at least go to your house to get the dirt?? Wait a minute, Ghesh picks up random dirt and slaps it on Sean’s wrist. The tattoo splutters a bit, then goes out, as if to say you’re close but not yet. It is currently midday, the party decides to go to the house to get the dirt then go to the markets.

Sean seems to know vaguely, possibly, maybe where to go. Ghesh spies a map store and goes in there with Folssa. The window is broken, and the cashier looks tired. Whats up? Standard catching register thieves etc etc. You want a map? She gets an updated [[South Edren Map.png|South Edren Map]], [[MAPDoncaster.png|Doncaster map]] and [[Velever Map.png|Velever map]]. 

Bud outside asks Faolan what he does with the tea cup - he’s still holding Stormrock’s cup. Where did your ears go? Uhhh same thing that makes you not look like metal. I don’t look like forgemother? Well Orrin wasn’t made of metal. No, I have four limbs. Well forgemother once had four limbs. They did? How did they lose them? Uhhhh well there’s multiple ways. There is?

Others come back out and they’re back to talking about tea cup. Bud tosses teacup into his chest cavity. It makes a wet grinding noise. Faolan goes are your insides meant to sound like that? Bud goes does your insides sound like that? I hope not. What is a map? tells you where you are? How? Tells you which way is north? What is north? North is north. What is west? Where the sun sets. What is the sun? THAT (point to orb in sky).

Party exits the dark blue area of [[MAPDoncaster.png|Doncaster Map]] - it is elevated from the other surrounding areas, and they can see this massive woven tree-root dome above the centre of the city protecting it. As the party leaves the outer wall they find these old dilapidated houses clustered together, one of them is Sean’s house. Its old and run down and seems like no one has been there in ages. Sean’s last memory of the place is him and constance playing in the dirt, with Greyson standing guard watching over the road. Sean has some negative feelings over the place but shakes them off and grabs the dirt and slaps it on. The tattoo glows a bright gold, similar to when Ghesh did the blood, but more of a Sean-esque yellow (vibes). The feeling of sitting by the front door, playing with constance, throwing his dad’s shoes and breathing the smell of the nightcaps on each other (it stains your breath pink and yellow) washes over. There’s sometimes the feeling of tension outside, which fades away, and the tattoo stops glowing.

Sean decides to go inside, the rest wait outside except bud, who follows. The floorboards are rotting, but somehow holds Bud’s weight. Sean goes into the downstairs area. It seems like it was used as a cellar, and part was magically warded as an air safe room. Most has been raided, but knowing Sean’s parents this was very much probably used for illegals. Sean leaves.

Gang goes to black markets - walk walk walk walk walk oh we’re here. It’s like 5pm. The gang don their disguises, and skim through the alleyways and over some rickety bridges. Sean directs the group towards a hole in the wall covered by a raggedy cloth into the market. The air smells like sulphur and acid reflux and burns the back of their nostrils - except for ghesh who doesn’t really notice, and bud who does not partake in the lung activities. The gang puts on the nightcap, and folssa sticks one on cocaine - she complains but hush my child i want you to breathe.

The gang agrees to meet up outside the hole at 6am if we get separated. Everyone follows Sean towards a magic item shop, which is his best guess of what could have something to overload the bird. As they enter, they sweep aside waves and waves of fabric trying to enter the stall. The longer they walk through the fabrics they begin to smear and swirl, and the longer they walk they get nauseous from the colours and swirls. The gang turns to look at each other to find one another, but realise they are alone. Everyone persists, and eventually they are spat out all in a row within a magic store. There are piles and piles of animals they have never seen before, there’s skulls (some are humanoid), and a weird engorged leech with a face (why you look at me like that). 

From the ceiling bud can see these bags, and he has the sense that something within them is very tasty. He pulls the cloth with the loksar symbol out and stares intently at the bag. What’s in there bud? I found terry. Random store guy goes that’s fine quality meat in there. Faolan can smell that its human meat. Bud goes to talk with the cashier asking to trade for it. I trade in money. i don’t have money. Convince your friends then they have money. The party tries to convince Bud its not actually Terry. Sean bargains with the shopkeep and goes thats my kid, leave him be. Don’t you have a beautiful loving family?

Sean shows the bird to the dodgy shopkeep - the shopkeep goes I will need to inspect it, but I can’t do it for free. 10 Gold. Sean continues to insult the guy (jesus christ my guy why) and the guy ups the price to 20. Why? I could explode man this could be a bomb you never know. Sean eventually accepts the 20. The guy goes to tinker with the bird. Ghesh manages to find some enchanted chalk and gemstones. 

Folssa takes Ghesh’s chalks and hagstones and goes to the counter. Using rustic hospitality Folssa chats with the guy and strikes up a deal. Apparently there was a guy who got paid 3000gp commission for setting up a greater teleportation circle for someone, pretty good pay. The guy doesn’t have any inks available but is looking to get some in - if Folssa comes back to deal with him again he can work out a bit of a discount. Folssa agrees.

Guy finished analysing bird, and says bird got slammed and had a magical overload. Recommends buying orb of antimagic, destroy it to make antimagic field that should clear up whatever message is left. Sean asks where to get antimagic orb, the guy has one. Eventually haggle to give the guy his disguise cloak, everfrost ale, and 2000gp for the orb.

The party leave, and after the curtains of fabric come out on the other side of the markets. Sean wants to go get high, ends up pissing Ghesh off as Ghesh thinks its stupid to go get high with a death robot with him. Group ends up splitting up, with Sean going to restock his supplies (weed) with bud, ghesh going with faolan to find a whisperward, and Folssa heading off to get potion materials. Ghesh gives Folssa 100gp to use.

Ghesh and Faolan go and track down the whisperward - old woman with dark blue robes and silver jewellery. She seems friendly enough, inviting Ghesh to sit down. She says the way this works is they barter for information, she wants unique interesting things for an old lady. Ghesh asks about Kurik. Kurikak Hatcher is his true name. He likes to say he is a cleric, he is not he is a healer. The lady says she can give more information, but she needs the barter. Ghesh offers information from the [[Demononomicon of Iggwilv]] she found in Torm, the lady is pleased by this.

Kurikak hatcher is a healer of dubious origin. Associated with “colourful” folk. Has recently found himself in the employment of Dudroskane clan - a newer development. Dudroskane are newer in general. Mr Hatcher is fly on the wall, does dubious jobs for very high price. He will refuse cases he thinks is beneath him, like simple cases. He is a bit pompous. She happens to know that Kurickak will be visiting an auction tomorrow evening on 5th Claymare street - its a bit of a exclusive event. You may need a token to get in, unless you have a family associated with a token. The lady pauses and says there is a particularly old and vulnerable fellow - Alys Rowntree - who has a token to the event. They frequent a bakery in the nicer areas of town - The Charm. 

Ghesh also asks about Gilthana Morwynn, the lady goes ooo that’s a pricy name. You’ll have to procure something of particular value.

As faolan is standing behind Ghesh, they’re glancing around and a prickle creeps up their spine. They are drawn to an alleyway, and already know what they’ll find. The spectre watches with a bulging yellow eye. She lifts her long clawed arm towards them, and with the other arm she starts to signal - the signal is from an old game they recognise. She makes a rabbit signal, and the outstretched arm sweeps across the crowd to a very specific spot - Faolan can pinpoint the general area, and notices someone duck away with a smoky black scarf - and the signal goes from rabbit to wolf. The message is clear - someone is following them.
