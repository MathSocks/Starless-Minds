### Session Summary:

Initiative: Hat guy, Sean

A bell echos through the arena as Hat Guy starts roaming the arena. When divario hears the bell, his entire body fills with adrenaline, and his only thought is survive. +2 on strength and -2 wisdom for 3 turns.
Divario notices in the ceiling there is a trapdoor, he goes up and out of the building. Sean notices nearby that a trapdoor on the room he is nearby absolutely shatters and a ram climbs through. They are all on the roof together now. The hunter hears the sound and heads that way. He immediately finds Sean and Constance, who scream when he suddenly appears. Hat man casts Hex on Sean, who gets disadvantage on Wisdom and extra damage against him for an hour. Sean manages to hit Hat Guy with ice knife for 3dmg.

Folssa and Lootra continue moving left. Faolan sees two deer go up and around the corner. Faolan scales up a nearby building, into Hat Guy’s line of sight, and casts mindspike. He hits for 18 damage, and Faolan always knows where he is. 

Ghesh examines the circle, and can see yhere is blood splattered into the circle of the sigil. It seems to fill the lines of the sigil. Where the blood touches the etchings, glints of magic rise upwards. It seems like its winding up to power something. Ghesh goes to a nearby chest - ghesh’s vision blacks out. There are two levers infront, a left and a right. They are the only options. There is no difference between them. Ghesh does left. On the other side of the wall, there is the sound of something activating, then vision back to normal. In chest there is jug, truth serum, assassin’s blood, 115 gold.

Odessa moves towards hat guy. Constance makes it back to human, and attacks hat guy with her longsword, she hits well. Divario fails a con save, but hits Hat Guy for 4 dmg. Hat Guy swipes at sean, sean is on 1 hit point. The blood sprayed from sean suspends in the air, and floats up hat guy’s arm. Sean books it.

Folssa gets to a chest, gets crawler mucus, 20 sling bullets, mace, book, gems worth 48 gold. Gart gets snapped by a bear trap as him and Turtan move. Faolan mind spikes Hat guy again, for 26dmg.

Ghesh hears the slow drip drip drip of blood, and sees drops of blood materialise and fall onto the circle. Back to real boy with con save. Ghesh casts magic missile on the stone, cracking it. The blood stops dripping, and the spell splutters slightly, but is still intact. 

Constance misses her longsword attack. Divario becomes a real boy, casts scorching rays, 22dmg. Hat guy attacks constance, hits and gets constance to 5hp. Sean heals himself
Folssa and Lootra go left, Folssa enters into one of the rune rooms, there’s a chest and altar - the altar does not look like a figure we’ve seen yet. It is a man with no skin holding a bowl with human soup. The muscle and flesh is melting like candle wax. Chest - truth serum, javelin of lightning, hammer, oil of taggit, adamantine chainmail, 1878 gold. 

Turtan gets stuck in a bear trap now, jesus christ. Faolan enters a small room with an altar. Tripping a tripwire, Faolan manages to avoid darts that shoot out from the wall. Ghesh casts grease on the sigil, the ritual circle wriggles out of the floor with these tiny stone legs under a dish and starts to run down the stairs. Ghesh casts firebolt, succeeds, and the ritual rock explodes. the blood from the stone disappears into thin air.

Odessa is still not real, still can’t do anything. Constance books it near Sean. Divario is a real boy, casts fireball for 56dmg. Hat guy hits divario for 20, divario hits back. Sean heals constance, becomes real boy, and opens chest.

Folssa sees the sigil, stabs it with his javelin, and the spell wavers but stays the same. Odessa can’t do shit. Ghesh finds a corpse, and scoops it up in her antlers. Constance and sean are nearby and think what the fuck.

Divario is not real, he hides. Hat guy attacks turtan with a fire greatsword, turtan kicks it. The blood is drawn into the sigils, and Folssa sees the blood pour into his sigil. As divario leaves, he hears hat guy say “Fucking finally”, the sigils go red with the blood, and Hat Guy transforms. He keels over and his clothes start to split apart. A young black dragon emerges from him.

Sean becomes a real boy. uses flame blade on the dragon and hits. Gart rages and hits while Turtan clocks out. Faolan becomes a real boy but misses his attack. 

Folssa dashes to the group, he applies a healer’s kit to Turtan and a healing potion to stop him from doing death saves. Ghesh walks in carrying the corpse. Odessa heads towards Faolan. The dragon does acid breath, catching folssa, ghesh, turtan and gart. Folssa stays up, ghesh turtan and gart are down.

Sean does land’s aid, gets full damage on the dragon (14dmg), also heals Folssa. Folssa goes to each of the downed and administers the healer’s kit (5 uses left) to stabilise everyone. Faolan can’t do anything, the rest are downed. Lootra comes and deals solid damage to the dragon. Odessa comes and gets a potion for ghesh, who gets 7hp. Constance comes and gives a potion to turtan, who gets 8hp. 11 potions left.

Dragon fails bite. Sean deals 5dmg. Dragon at 52hp. Ki point for 2 action 2 unarmed strikes. two attacks with lightning javelin, two unarmed strikes with final nat 20. Folssa obliterates the dragon. 2900 xp from dragon, 14350 total. We are level 6. Folssa gives potion to ghesh and gart at end. 

Divide and conquer, Ghesh and lootra go find key, folssa, gart and sean go to bottom left, odessa, divario, turtan and faolan go top left.

Folssa, Gart and Constance are at glyph. Sean nearly goes in, but hears a wall collapse, and within it, two people can be seen. One wears a matching sigil to the one that Sean was given my Blanke at the beginning of torm. Sean follows, they’re saying things like this level is really annoying, etc.

Folssa, Constance and Gart do a comp of who can beat the shit out of the glyph the most. The thing runs away

Odessa, Divario, and Faolan immediately blows up the thing before it runs.

Lootra shows ghesh the hole with the key, she grabs it. They head to the door, and test the area for traps. When they find nothing, they laugh at their paranoia. Inside find a spear called Ruin’s Wake. They take it and head out

As Sean prepares to follow the people, a running sigil followed by Folssa bolts past. Sean ignores them and goes straight to talk to the strange people. He makes sure to show a touch of his necklace. The guy asks reluctantly if Sean is part of the [[Verity Order]]. Sean successfully convinces the guy they have met before. Its a man and woman duo. They ask Sean if he knows if Arlo is disposed of already, plus something about its annoying to get those apostles down. Sean says he doesn’t know. He then tries to subtly flirt with them to keep them around. Folssa runs after the sigil and manages to whack out its legs under them. The people go shit shit shit there’s people on the level, we gotta go. Sean tries to go along with them. They say why don’t you have a guide stone, Sean says he does, but he doesn’t think its the most important thing in his pocket right now. Blah blah blah. There’s “bastards” up top that are swarming everywhere, breeding everywhere, blocking our paths, etc. The “bloke” wasn’t happy?

When they try to leave, Sean wraps his arms around the bloke’s shoulders, and subtly tries to take the guide stone. he nearly gets it until the guy goes wtf are you doing, he’s tryna get my fucking guide stone. Sean defends himself saying wtf do you mean, etc.

When Folssa takes out the last sigil, he hears a noise signalling the level is complete. 
The duo go shit they finished the level, we gotta go. 
Everyone slowly notices we aren’t alone, and coalesce around the area sean is
Sean and the guy (his name is Elel) start arguing, Elel picks up that Sean is a tower climber

Ghesh sees the guide stone, there is a man draped in robes engraved, his neck ending in a stump. Ghesh recognises the person from her torm vision. Sean and Ghesh agree through message they need to get the stone. Folssa scales ghesh and whispers to her whats up, what do you need. He offers to run and get the stone for her. Divario pipes in about getting the rock, the woman notices, and she yells out. Ghesh manages to pin down the guy while Folssa dodges the woman and gets the stone from the guy, jumping onto a roof out of reach. The lady eventually negotiates that we don’t know how to use it, what if we get you out of here with us when arlo the dragon reawakens, then we go from there. After negotiation, the party agrees to go, and they leave down the hallway made by the stone.
