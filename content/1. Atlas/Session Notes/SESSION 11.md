### Timeline of Events
After leaving fenfyx, the wagon continues on to a town called Tarnsby. In the distance, we see a small group of 4 people yelling between each other - they seem to be grilling this one specific person. The person responds with blah blah "We don't even know the spell works". The guy who was speaking - stomach caves in, building pressure in the head, and the person blows up. This repeats for each person, until all that's left is the upright bodies.

Ghesh goes to observe the bodies without touching. Heat radiates off their bodies, they're still standing upright in death. Divario throws up. Folssa pulls a big long stick and pokes the bodies, the muscles are completely stiff (TETANUS). Ghesh casts detect magic - there are heavy rolls of magic thats hot but starting to cool. It feels complex and layered. Ghesh pokes to try lift the arm, and ghesh sees a golden script on their arm. It looks pretty much identical to the party. There's a single symbol that's different, but it looks like a group identifier. The group is two dwarves, a gnome, and a dragonborn. They are missing a member. Faolan casts detect magic, can't really sense any particular spell that they might have been talking about. Ghesh then casts detect magic on the party - nothing on folssa except the spell, sean has soft, lush magic that smells like fresh cut weed, divario feels darker and smells of iron, faolan has this eerie heavy, tiny black-hole magic feeling. Sean detects magic on ghesh, ghesh feels eclectic with many signals and jarring, main magic is pleasant with electric crackle, though there's a few other signals that you can't really pin point, one of them feels hot white. Sean does a history check for the white hot, but kinda feels like a cleric sean dated one time. Sean tells ghesh her vibe is good, but she smells like sean's ex (haha, nah it's just cleric-ish). 

Divario says we are on the run for mass genocide, lets not raid the corpses. Ghesh looks divario in the eye and stick her hand in one of the pockets. She gets a little shock from it, like two fabrics rubbing against each other. She finds house keys. Ghesh checks more pockets, still getting that spark. Divario, Folssa and Faolan start walking away. Sean stays on babysitting ghesh duty. Reena walks out of the wagon like WTF. She is concerned it's contagious, or that the field is a mindfield (SKY-MINDFIELDS GHESH, WHAT IF). Sean and Reena travel to the town in the wagon, reena holds onto her head saying "it's just comfortable". Ghesh still raids the pockets - in one dwarf pocket is a notebook of groceries, supplies, etc, and a sketch of him with another guy and a kid. There's also 5 gold. Ghesh returns the notebook. Second dwarf has candle, picture, piece of chalk and rusty nail. The rest of the pockets are random generic trash. Ghesh then follows the party.

The party enters the town. There's no festivities going on, it's just a chill day. A guard gives divario a funny look - folssa sticks his hat on divario and drapes him arms around him. Folssa and Sean head to the stall - a dragonborne is living it up, with a set-up kitchen cooking things and he's making it a show. He has a long flat head like a crocodile with an indian accent. He asks if sean and folssa want food, they agree, and he whips food around the table in a potato dish. He says no payment, just remember the name Ranjet. He is from Ecrusia, and found it boring. Conversation drifts to where we're going, he says Torm city is very interesting, some people love change and some hate it. The food ranjet cooks is amazing. He says he might hit up Veritas dam sooner or later (south of torm city, a popular town). Sean and Folssa go to find the rest of the party to bring them to try the food.

Faolan, Ghesh and Divario head into the church - timber two story with grey shingles, with paintings of a local religious figure named eldath. There are people milling about and reflecting on the paintings. A friendly looking gnome greets the group. She says a nice old lady named Rollan runs the church. Eldath visited the church a few hundred years ago herself, she's a minor water spirit. She got ties with mystra, but she does her own thing. Mystra's temple (on top of Mystra's cup), is a temple south east of Torm city. The tower of torm first level is open to the public, but is closed up tight at night. They leave the church.

The party meets back up. (Folssa passes Cocaine to Reena, Reena is cocaine-approved). Some divine intervention from loreal paris guides the party to the general store called the cat-tail which is owned by a dwarf fellow. The party buys a map which ghesh and divario fight over. Ghesh says to sean the ducks will not be saved if we follow divario, sean ignores the commend and goes to smoke the weed. Sean has a bad trip, tries passing the keys to Reena. Reena wants to have some weed, and has a good trip. Both of them try to drive. Folssa ends up wrangling Reena out and Folssa gets into the drivers seat.

The team meets a fork in the road and take a bet on a short cut. The team cut their time in half. The team travel on for two days. 

At one point, Faolan gets a feeling over their shoulder, and they glance out into the field. A haggard, rotting wolf head hangs over the field. A vaguely human body unfurls from the body. White braids mix in with her wolf fur. Faolan remembers their braids, thick and heavy on their back, and tight on their scalp. Faolan just looks out in the general direction. The others don't see anything, except for Ghesh. Ghesh sees a figure just standing there, no details but its clearly got Faolan's attention. Faolan jumps a bit at Ghesh's approach, ghesh asks do you see that, Faolan says no. The more faolan watches the figure, the more she seems familiar. She is slowly getting closer, and there is a nagging feeling that they've seen the person before. Faolan slightly raises their hand, and the figure flickers and disappears, but Faolan knows she will be back, and she will be closer. Ghesh asks if Faolan got a response from the grass. Faolan just gives a disgruntled squint. Ghesh quietly ask if they know what that was, Faolan stops and ever so slightly shake their head. Ghesh asks if this has happened before, Faolan doesn't respond. 

Sean was drug tripping, Folssa was DUI, Faolan has gone back to the hammock and Ghesh is journalling. Divario pretends to know what Ghesh is writing. Divario asks if he can borrow Ghesh's cloak, Ghesh says if anything happens Divario will become living compost with the amount of dirt she will stuff into him. Divario returns the cloak. Divario gets some cloth and water and heads to Faolan who's in the hammock. Divario asks if he can clean faolan's blood-stained clothes and injuries to reduce the scarring. Faolan reluctantly agrees, and divario works his way through the shitty bandages, avoiding eye contact. Divario apologies about what happened, Faolan says its fine you can't control what happened. Divario leaves the water and rags with Faolan, and leaves Faolan to themself.
