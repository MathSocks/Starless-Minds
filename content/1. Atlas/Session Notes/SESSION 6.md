### Timeline of Events

#### Out of the cave

The party moves onwards in the grassy plains in [[South Edren]]. A [[Rickety Shack]] is visible in the distance. [[Ghesh]], [[Bodie Stomart]] and [[Folssa]] head towards the hut. [[Odessa Velle]] goes back to the [[Gallow's Arena]] entrance to set up a minor illusion to keep away anyone trying to follow the party. [[Divario]] and [[Faolan]] are left behind, and have a brief discussion about what happened in [[Westtown]].

#### At the [[Rickety Shack]]

[[Odessa Velle]], [[Divario]] and [[Faolan]] meet everyone at the [[Rickety Shack]]. [[Bodie Stomart]] goes to lie down with [[Ghesh]] curled in his arms. [[Folssa]] curls up in [[Divario]]'s arms and both lay down to sleep. [[Faolan]] goes to sleep in a corner.

> [!delmessa]-
> [[Odessa Velle]] asks [[Ghesh]] if they can speak. One of [[Ghesh]]'s kobolds follow [[Odessa Velle]] outside, where they stand awkwardly in the rain. [[Odessa Velle]] reluctantly agrees that [[Ghesh]] won the fight at the [[Gallow's Arena]], and that she won the right to be turned back into a dragonborne. However, [[Odessa Velle]] asks in return that [[Ghesh]] convinces [[Bodie Stomart]] to go back to [[Lexafell Academy]], saying "He has a better head on his shoulders than either of us combined, and I don't care to see it blown off." She also tells [[Ghesh]] that the werewolf needs to be sorted out soon - either by curing it or killing it. [[Ghesh]] tries to argue back, but the conversation soon dies off. [[Odessa Velle]] restarts the conversation by asking if there are any questions [[Ghesh]] wants answers to:

> [!Faolan]-
> [[Faolan]] wakes up early. The rain has stopped, but thick fog wafts around the shack. [[Faolan]] feels drawn to getting up and heading outside, where he sits on the edge of the edge of the patio. A figure of the rotting, twisted head of a dog with a cloth over its eyes shifts in and out of view. [[Faolan]] suddenly recalls the wagon chase from a few nights ago, but doesn't know why. [[Faolan]] has the feeling that the dog head can see them despite the cloth, and that it looks at them longingly, almost hungrily, before it disappears back into the fog.

#### Sean's Return

[[Divario]] stirs, shifts [[Folssa]] off himself and tucks his hair behind his ear as [[Divario]] gets up and wanders outside. He immediately trips on [[Faolan]] in the doorway, and sits next to him, looking out at the mist. From the distance, [[Faolan]] and [[Divario]] hear the echo of a ghastly scream, and two figures can be seen running through the fog to the party's location. [[Divario]] leaps up and bangs his broken sword against his shoulder armour to create a racket that wakes the party. He is then immediately tackled by [[Ghesh]].

[[Sean]] and [[Reena]] emerge from the [[Gallow's Arena]] passageway and find themselves in a grassy plain. They head towards a [[Rickety Shack]] in the distance, and can hear a horrendous banging. [[Reena]] is ready to cast a spell, while [[Sean]] preps his crossbow as they approach. [[Odessa Velle]] heads outside the shack, fingerguns ready, and yells out "I'm not against being acquainted with the title murderess". [[Sean]] recognises [[Odessa Velle]]'s voice and runs towards her, which prompts a warning shot. [[Ghesh]] rushes out from the Shack, and upon recognising [[Sean]], shouts "He's with us!" at [[Odessa Velle]]. The rest of the party then comes out to greet [[Sean]]. He introduces [[Reena]] to the party, who looks like she wants to be anywhere else. She whispers to [[Sean]] "Between you and me I don't really want to hang out with murderers". After some more conversation, [[Reena]] agrees to fetch [[Sean]]'s wagon, and leaves.

#### Family Meeting

[[Divario]] calls a family meeting, and begs the party to not introduce ourselves as crooks each time we go to a new town. He also starts to recap to [[Sean]] what happened last night. [[Sean]] exchanges his view as well. [[Faolan]] being a werewolf is revealed to [[Sean]], who rolls a history check. He recalls the following:

- [[Werewolves]] are known as a boogie-man type of bedtime story
- Most stories are inaccurate and overexaggerated
- When you think of [[Werewolves]], you think of mass murder
  [[Divario]] asks [[Faolan]] if he knows of any other [[Werewolves]]. [[Faolan]] doesn't know/think so

#### Convincing Bodie

[[Ghesh]] goes back to [[Bodie Stomart]] and notices that he seems visibly upset. [[Ghesh]] grabs [[Bodie Stomart]]'s hair and guides him away from [[Odessa Velle]] and behind the shack, and asks him what's wrong. [[Bodie Stomart]] says [[Odessa Velle]] is telling him to go back to [[Lexafell Academy]] Academy because he will be safer there. [[Bodie Stomart]] swears to [[Ghesh]] that he is useful and doesn't need to go back to the academy. [[Ghesh]] tells [[Bodie Stomart]] that he is useful, but that [[Odessa Velle]] is right and he should go home. [[Bodie Stomart]] then tries to convince [[Ghesh]] to come back with him. [[Ghesh]] says she can't, but she will meet [[Bodie Stomart]] at [[Lexafell Academy]] after [[Torm]]. [[Bodie Stomart]] argues back with [[Ghesh]], stating that no one comes out of the Tower of [[Torm]], and that [[Ghesh]] has already died once. After some more back and forth, [[Ghesh]] finally convinces [[Bodie Stomart]] to go back to [[Lexafell Academy]] so long as [[Ghesh]] doesn't set foot in [[Torm]]. [[Ghesh]] agrees, knowing that she will have to break that promise.

[[Odessa Velle]] takes a step back out of the building as the roof of the shack shatters from the energy of the spell
