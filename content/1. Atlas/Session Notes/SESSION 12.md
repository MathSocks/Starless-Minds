### Timeline of Events
The party long rests. Torm city comes into full view - it is so tall it disappears into the clouds. The city is rings around the tower. The circus troupe is in the distance, about a day off. The party has free rein to explore the city.

Eastern Slums and Western drogs are lower-income are for illicit stuff. Greyfair and upper bales are fancy areas. Western docks is the taverns for people to get shitfaced. South city, tanna's crest and iron row is middle class. Frid hill is middle class/religious sect, good for info.

Folssa and ghesh go to Frid Hill. Divario and Faolan go to Tanna's crest. Sean goes to Western Docks with Reena.

Frid hill: Ghesh and Folssa chat a bit about South Edren language. The first building they see is a tourist guide sort of place that advertises tours. They continue to the biggest beautiful bookshop they have ever seen. Its full of snaking hallways with two levels. Ghesh is looking for books on Zac and Mystra, Torm, and changling/magic/multiple auras type stuff. There are large sections on Torm and Mystra, nothing on Zac, and a few books on gollum creation, nothing on apple juice of immortality, and some books on clerics. She finds a book called "Featherwud's guide to cleric life", which is a generic book on clerics. She flicks through it and sees some basic spells. Folssa notices that the people in the area often have a lot of big tattoos - Ghesh (low perception) sees that the tattoos don't look the same as the party's, and doesn't seem to have magic to it, but notices that its odd that so many people have them. 

Western Docks: Sean and Reena race to the docks. Sean meanwhile stops into a post office to drop off letters to Orrin and family. They continue to the docks and head into the first tavern they see. Sean bets 5gp that he can drink more than Reena. The bartender looks very concerned. They get advantage on strength and charisma, but disadvantage on Dex for it. Sean takes the drinks better than Reena. Sean then asks the bartender for some lighter drinks. Sean asks for one of each liquor (95 gold), which reena also gets.

Tanna's Crest: Divario and Faolan decide to window shop. They go find a blacksmith, a human looking fellow. Divario asks the blacksmith to tell him about his blade. The smith says its a bloodshed blade - the carvings at the top (such as the invoking rune). They leave the sword with the blacksmith to study. Faolan asks if they have any quarterstaffs or crossbows, they only have javelins. The pair leaves, and Divario takes Faolan to buy new socks.

Ghesh finds some studies by [[Morwynn Gilthana]] and [[Brustoul Mudmier]]. Brustoul seems to be trying to make magic more accessible to the common man. Morwynn has been writing and producing texts for over two hundred years. The most recent study he did was about 50 years ago on teleportation circles. She finds a book on Ecrusia - Another continent with deep magical history and a large dragonborn population. It is also apparently one of the few places where actual dragons can be found. Folssa goes around the halls looking for secret entrances, slowly pissing off the librarian. The pair eventually leave the library.

Sean and Reena proceed to get drunker and drunker. Reena was on the dance floor, now on the floor floor. From across the room, two guys yell over to Reena in recognition. The bracken brothers, blonde-red and well built, stumble over drunkenly. They immediately take to Sean, saying they're from Velever. Sean goes back to the bartender, who Sean rizzes up and they go off to shag.

Divario goes back to the blacksmith. The rune on the hilt means you add your constitution to damage made by the weapon (like adding your strength of will). The blacksmith says the damage was made by blunt force trauma - either hit by a better weapon, or wielder didn't know what to do with it. The blacksmith and Divario swap names (his name is Hamil), and Divario confesses that he lost his memories and he doesn't know anything about the sword. Hamil asks divario what he drinks, Divario goes on a rant that he doesn't drink, Hamil says he was just asking if he wanted to get coffee. They chat a bit more, Divario lets loose he's travelling with his two kids, his sister and his husband, and Hamil is like jesus you're married ok byeee. Divario walks off depressed. Faolan comes back to see him talking with a pigeon.

Folssa goes to discuss with an old guy at the museum. The old guy says the city was built around the Tower - there used to be a castle there with a small community, but during the birth of torm the castle transformed. The tower used to have multiple floors open to the public, but after a few disappearances it was closed. The tower likes to gobble those who are too curious. The castle on ashen hill is the current government building, used to be a castle for kings but they died out. He also says Tanna's crest is good for adventurers, and western docks is where the sailors come in. The guy used to be a sailor, doing a lot of supply runs from Velever to South Edren, sometimes ecrusia - it is magic heavy there, you can smell it everywhere. He also confirms that dragonborn often travel in flocks, and that loksar is in war with north edren. (his name is Gruk Milton)

Sean has come back from fucking the bartender. He is chatting with the braken brothers while reena snoozes. Sean leaves the tavern, proceeds to head to another bar. Someone walks up to him and offers him a drink, buying Sean a shot of liquor. This man's name is Tam, how are you man? Sean wanders outside to the deck over the lake, where he sees people diving into the lake. Sean half climbs the ladder, knocks his shin, and badly flips into the lake. Someone yells LAKE and five people jump after him.

Faolan starts walking somewhat towards Folssa and Ghesh, where they see Odessa Velle ahead. Divario also sees her. She is heading somewhat towards the western docks or upper balles. Faolan goes on to find Ghesh and Folssa. They are surprised to see them alone - they try say nothing, but fold and said they saw Odessa and divario was following. Folssa goes off to find him.

Sean is confronted by a bouncer who tries to get him out of the lake. Sean walks out like a sad wet cat. Back in the bar, Sean bounces off someone, and a piece of paper is stuck to his clothes. The paper says: Torm. Tomorrow. 4pm. Sean sees someone hastily take off and tries to follow them. The guy moves faster when he notices sean following. Sean bumps into a person, hits a building, and falls on his face. The person is gone. Sean proceeds to stumble into another bar and sits down somewhere

Divario continues to follow Odessa, who ducks into the alchemist store. Divario ducks into the store, sticking to the back wall and going behind a divider. Odessa is just shopping around. Divario plays off as window shopping, talking to the shopkeeper, and Odessa recognises the voice but doesn't look up. Divario asks for something like a relaxant potion. The shop owner redirects him to a music shop in Upper Balles. He also asks for something for his alchemist friend, and the shopkeeper gives him a bottle of alchemist fire. Divario asks Odessa out for coffee, opens the door for her but slams it on her. Odessa takes Divario to this classy little cafe. They sip their coffee's in silence. Divario asks Odessa for help, and asks her what she knows about Fey. He wants to fix what happened, and wants to know where to find the Fey, and wants her to teach Divario some things. He also asks her about werewolves, and if there's any way to stop it or prevent it. Odessa responds with she is not familiar with any way of curing a werewolf, which means you have to make one. The problem has to be solved. If Divario wants her help and they can't cure it, Divario needs to consider the 'ultimate choice'. Odessa has rented a room in the upper balles and advises Divario to do the same.

Folssa runs into Divario, who collapses to his knees and hugs him. Folssa guides him back to Ghesh and Faolan. Ghesh receives the alchemist fire thing, and the two have a big bear hug. Folssa goes with Divario to get a room in lower balles, ghesh and faolan wander, and sean is still drunk somewhere.
