### Timeline of Events

The group has bought a property on some land. The old owner - Harron Olbuck - Halfling, very spherical, comes out and address the group. Back and forth, the group discovers that Tollund tricked Flat white into paying 60% of the deed, swifty paying the other 40%. Haybale contributed a bottle cap.

Flat white gets given the keys to main build, arena, some outdoor buildings, tavern, etc.

Rumblerats used to own the property. Harron inherited it from a family member who died from contact with the weird substances (_drugs_) in the house - they were quickly swept and snorted up. The contract is signed on a napkin between Harron and Tollund

Tollund passes napkin to Marco, marco sniffs the napkin and sets off into the house. The others follow. Marco follows the scent through the tavern, in and around rooms, through the stable, just ploughing through rooms until he gets to the arena, where he sniffs cocaine under the floor. He starts scratching at it and yelling.

Swifty looks at the decor of the buildings. The two main are sturdy, but very ugly - no sparkles, no glitter, they’re just sad. She comes across marco, who is still yelling at the floor. He unsheaths his greataxe and smashes the pommel into the floor. It doesn’t break but it sounds very hollow. Swifty is intrigued, and takes the greataxe and smashes it harder. A hollow echo rumbles and in the corner something clicks. Swifty and marco race to the corner.

Haybale goes to the tavern. There’s a large storeroom with all the ale cleared out, but the junk remaining. Haybale does find a single bottle of Divario bourbon under some floor boards behind the bar - they leave it. In store room there’s some trap items:

- 3 large nets
- 5 barrels
- 5 explosive runes
- 50 hemp rope
- one bag of something
- Something else
  Haybale then follows the screams to the indoor arena

Flat white takes a Rufus his metal fairy - he took his from the treasury - and sees if he can find anything of value. Rufus acts like a gruff crackhead. Rufus sets off trying to find metal, and the two head towards the outdoor arena. Rufus senses metal in the water - Flat white gives a sugar cube in response (oh yeah thats the good shit). Flat white sees some gold in water - he pans for it, thinking it is peaceful, then a scream knocks him over and the gold tumbles away. He manages to catch a single nugget, its like 500 bucks.

Tollund goes to the stables. 3 investigation - _once there were horses_. Bog man makes five hay bed in the stable.

Marco beats swifty to the corner - there’s a trapdoor leading to crawlspace. Marco dives in first, the crawlspace leads to under the arena. Bits of light stream through the boards, looks like a place where you’d rig a fight. There is a light dusting of cocaine on the ground, a couple ropes, some other trapdoors, etc, and a dead body. Also a halfling, maybe the dead relative from earlier. There is some cocaine dusting the body, marco sweeps the cocaine from the floor and the body and shoves it in his pocket. He then drags the body out of the crawlspace by the leg. Swifty freaks out, Flat white hears and comes. He goes wtf at the body, calls family meeting. Haybale and tollund go woah. Flat white explores the crawlspace, finds levers and buttons, and decides hey lets pull these. First level sucks the floor around haybale’s foot. Second button whacks a railing into tollunds head and he’s out, Marco is sitting on floor and third lever licks Marco’s ankle. Marco licks the floor in return. Flat white comes back out and is greeted with whatever tf happened. Marco offers flat white the cocaine he pocketed, flat white wraps it up in marco’s hankerchief and gives it to Rufus so they can sell later. Jesse pink-of-man reminds Flat White about the Redrun tourney, where the gangs of velever competed against one another - came in two halves including a part where they compete in games. Flat white goes hey guys, hear me out, we advertise Marco (the strongest) with come fight the marvelous marco polo, and we rig the fights from beneath. We just gotta fix up the place - flat white calls Rufus, who calls Lord business. They bargain with how much of a loan we can get. Ask for 10k, return 20k, lord business goes mmm 7k for 20k back, we go but we said we’d double what you invest, he goes yeah you said 10k so you think you can make 20k, i want the 20k. We get a deal for 7k.

Party goes to bed in the hay beds. Group wakes up, and rufus the genie fairy brings a list of staff and games. We pick three games per half, with a bouncer, a technical manager and a game host for each half. The group decides haybale whisky and swifty will do explosions and glitter to draw in the crowd. Tollund points out the random stranger with us - he says oh I’m here to uhhh be your advertising specialist. His name is Menath. He goes along with whisky and swifty.
