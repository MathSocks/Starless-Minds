The team stands outside Torm. People are milling around, coming and going. It's very difficult to sense any sort of other magic due to the cloying effect of torm's magic. Its clouding the magic senses of everyone else. Faolan can pick out Odessa's perfume in the area, so they can tell she's inside. They also notice that the people around the tower are very eclectic and multicultural - there's a lot of people dressed in yellow and gold, with gold masks that seem to be like some sort of security. Sean says to faolan in the non-rudest way possible "can you sniff out my ducks". Divario turns around, points at the tower, and yells "SOMEONE'S ABOUT TO HIT THE TOWER". Everyone starts screaming and yelling as they run in random directions. Most of the party manage to dodge people, but ghesh gets tackled to the ground with people stepping all over, stepping on her tail, etc. Folssa pries the legs of anyone whp walks his way open and dives through, nimbly dodging the crowd as they fall over behind him. Divario manages to pick up Ghesh. Folssa scales up a random guy's legs, jumps head to head in the crowd, and jumps onto ghesh like he's surfing. Faolan gets knocked over as well, while Sean twink-shuffles towards the entrance. The guards start going through the crowd to diffuse the situation.

The party approach wide ornate doors - 10 people tall, castle like doors - and the room beyond absolutely massive and very empty. Sean places the wagon and enlarges the token. He goes inside and heads to his duck nest, where he gets two sticks and hands them to Faolan. Faolan sniffs the sticks - someone in the area smells vaguely of the ducks, but they since left the tower in the 9/11 confusion. The party hears odessa run up the stairs into the room, looking dishevelled from the stampede. We all just hear "WHAT". After bickering over what the best plan for causing a distraction isn't, Sean shrinks the wagon and goes for a wander around the tower.

Folssa starts booking it up and down the hall - it takes 2.5 turns to travel the length of this place. There are two statues at either end.

Sean goes wandering. It feels like he's being observed. Sean uses druidcraft to amplify the breeze through the door and see if the presence alters at all. Sean notices off to both sides of the room, the breeze seems to drift into hallways on both sides - secret tunnels.

Ghesh looks around at the religion texts in the room. They're all very focused on Torm - the god of courage and self-sacrifice. Ghesh moves towards Odessa who's at the front statue with Folssa. Ghesh asks Odessa what she's trying to study here - Odessa says she's looking for places where people can disappear into, because people have to go somewhere if they're going missing. Odessa starts wandering around the statue and remarks how dare people graffiti on the statues - "The statues get a little quirky at night". Ghesh picks up a shit ton of magic embedded in the statue.

Folssa notices that the statue in the entrance looked like a lone knight with a fully decked out suit of armour. He seemed well off - a lord's knight - some sort of financial backing. Beautiful armour with intricate decorations. A bit of an androgenous look. Very solitary and lonely looking. It leans forward - if you track the eyes, it looks directly at the statue at the other end, one hand on the sword and other on the chest. Very forlorn looking and resolved.

Folssa bolts to the other end. The statue at the other end is a group of multiple people with a key figure at the top. It's a collection of servants pushing up against a throne, where a figure (a lord or king of some kind) stands with a flat halo behind him. His face has been defiled to a blank slate. This statue feels as dead as a statue can be. It's pointed upwards - there's a really intricate painting on the ceiling. Along the rim, there's hundred and hundreds of statues crowded and jostled together. They're in fairly formal gear, whispering to each, and some are staring uncomfortably down at people below. There are paintings on the glass, and in places where there's no paintings you can see yourself.

Odessa says that the tower used to be a castle, but during Torm's birth the castle converted into the tower.

Faolan and Divario watch the chaos outside. There are guards patrolling around outside trying to contain the chaos. Faolan can still smell the duck guy. A young man says "Heyyy sean, haven't seen you in a long time". This is not the duck person. The person is middle-road sort of guy, very familiar with Sean, close body language, hand still on arm. "We'll catch up soon" and Faolan drags Divario out of there. The duck person seems to have circled around and is heading back towards torm.
