### Session Summary:
Sean is a bit more lucid but still under the influence. Folssa and Sean enter into the bathhouse where Folssa said Elel may be. Turns out he’s actually there to Folssa’s dismay. They cannonball over near the guy, who immediately goes wtf my day is ruined. Bluntly questioning the guy, Elel goes unless the pendant is up my ass, i don’t have it. The duo leave him alone.

They head over to divario and gart. Divario is yapping Gart’s ear off about the wonder of folssa james quillback. After some back and forth, Gart and Sean exchange some purple pot, gart goes you are a brother, i got some uhh errands to run. After he leaves, Divario asks are we getting the pendant, and the group briefly discuss it.

Ghesh knocks on the door to the room Odessa is in. Ghesh asks if odessa is alright, odessa says im not the one accosted by a group member. Ghesh goes yeahhhhh im not impressed. Odessa gets a bit angry, how dare he talk to you like that, i should have whacked him on the side of his head. If he had any idea who you were, there would not be a word, its absolutely ridiculous. Ghesh finds how worked up she’s getting adorable, odessa does not appreciate the word adorable, choose again. Powerful? Yes i like that one better. After a little back and forth where Ghesh apologises for taunting the wraith a second time, and odessa thanks ghesh for saving her life. They end up discussing what they would do with Ghesh’s body if she falls apart - sew her together, the limbs move around, maybe the hands could hold the head, or keep Ghesh’s head on a mini throne with a baby gate. Better yet they could lasso ghesh’s head and put it in the bag of holding, or stuffed into a crate with a cushion. Ghesh asks if she wants to go to the baths, Odessa says yes. They get there, and go goddamn it theres people. They go in the farthest corner.

Sean sees Odessa and Ghesh walk past. Sean wants to stop third wheeling, but ghesh indicates he may third wheel here too. Divario and Folssa notice as well, Divario ends up leaving - he feels guilty to ghesh - and folssa waves at ghesh but stays where he is. Ghesh gives a look going what is with divario, folssa shrugs. Folssa starts doing laps, while Sean plays mermaids.

Divario gets to the house. Faolan is clocked tf out. Divario decides to go talk to Daffodint. He finds him in this courtyard under the only tree in the area near his office. Divario tries to come quietly, but clocks his toe completely. They start talking, and divario wants to start the quest. He tries convince Daffodint to give him the quest info, and he goes and does it himself. Daffodint is like wtf no, you wanna do this dangerous mission on your own? I would have sent someone myself if it were that easy. Divario is like come on man wtf. Do you have any basic bitch quests then? Daffodint is like no man. Divario goes to find something else to blow off steam. 

Sean and Folssa descend into water fight - odessa and ghesh watch humourously. Sean starts winning, having jumped on Folssa in the deep end. Folssa scales sean’s legs, Sean screams. Ghesh and odessa are placing bets - they bet folssa. Folssa gets onto Sean’s shoulders and pulls sean’s ears. Sean manages to hook his arm and tosses him off. Ghesh and odessa are like goddamn it folssa what the hell. Sean and folssa shake on it with ggs, but Folssa doesn’t take the loss and tackles Sean again. Sean runs away to the edge of the pool to catch his breath, Folssa stalks him. Sean goes to jump out of the pool, makes it to the tiles, but is suddenly yoinked down before he can scream. Eventually everyone at the pools head back to the house.

As the party sleeps, Ghesh experiences memory (memory debt), Sean experiences [[Boys Don't Cry]], and Divario experiences [[Saddles]]

Divario, Ghesh, Odessa and Sean wake up. Odessa hogs the bathroom. Divario, Sean and Ghesh are all in the living room. Ghesh and Divario don’t talk. Sean talks a little with Ghesh until Ghesh leaves the room. Divario and Sean start talking lightly, Sean goes oh god ghesh hates me she left the room, divario is like no she hates me. After back and forth, Divario goes i wanna talk to ghesh, and bursts open the door to hit ghesh and odessa square in the face as they were listening in. Ghesh goes back in the room to talk to divario while Sean goes with odessa after begging her not to be left with them. They try wake up Folssa, who is clocked out. Sean talks mermaids with him, folssa rolls over and goes yes i am a mermaid. 

Ghesh and divario start talking. Divario starts out with apologising for how he acted, Ghesh goes no no what else have you done. You were shitty yesterday, what did you do or say. Divario reluctantly confesses that he argued with Folssa, “but its ok he seemed ok”, Ghesh rips into him. She seems mildly surprised that Folssa doesn’t want his memories when Divario says thats what they argued about, but they have a full on argument over whether or not they support that. The convo devolves a bit and deescalates when Ghesh says to Divario you have your own choice here, you are currently half the man your past self was, pick yourself up and decide what you want for yourself. They go back and forth a bit more, Divario asks at one point, “do you trust me”

Folssa has woken up to sweet mermaid nothings. All three of folssa, odessa and sean overhear the convo - Folssa mutters under his breath “i didn’t realise my memories were such a controversial topic”. Divario ends up busting into the room, Odessa and Folssa immediately pull a im-not-looking, “and thats the secret to a good haircare routine”, isn’t that right sean, Sean agrees “yes yes i like hair” but is staring straight at divario obviously. 

Divario wanders off temporarily, while Sean, Ghesh, Folssa and Odessa go to the balcony and have a smoke. Faolan wakes up to the smell of nicotine. Ghesh asks faolan how they slept, faolan says fine, they’ve slept in worse places. Sean and faolan compare the worst places they remember sleeping in, the list including places like alleyways and bars. Folssa ends up pulling ghesh aside, asking if he can talk to her. They go inside and the pair chat a bit about divario and are they ok after their respective interactions with him. Folssa confides with ghesh about his interaction, revealing firstly how divario tried to get him to promise him not to leave. Ghesh is quite angry at that, her trust goes down further. Folssa also tells how they argued over how folssa doesn’t want his memories and divario thinks thats cowardly. Ghesh reassures him that he can do what he wants with his memories, she doesn’t think its cowardly and to do what he thinks is best for himself regardless of others’ opinions. Folssa says thats exactly what i plan on doing, but thanks for the reassurance. He also tells her not to do anything rash with the info, he just wanted her to know.
