The party start heading for the hills surrounding the [[south Edren]] fields. They enter a place called the [[Howling Hills]], where they are battered by extreme winds which they cannot hear each other over. In the distance, they see a short, stout [[Farmer]] in thick clothing herding some massive goats over a chasm.

[[Ghesh]] hurls [[Folssa]] towards the [[Farmer]], but underestimates the strength of the wind and [[Folssa]] nearly falls off the cliff, and is holding on by his hands.
