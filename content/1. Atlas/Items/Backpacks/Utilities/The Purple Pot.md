### Properties/Details:

A strange looking ground leaf that can be rolled into blunts. A player's experience with the pot will change depending on the trajectory of the 'trip'.

Players will roll every so often to see the trajectory of the trip. This will change depending on the environment (an unsettling/scary environment would make a bad trip more likely). Baseline is under 10 is a bad trip, and above 10 is a good trip.


| Good Trip Stats                                                  | Bad Trip Stats                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| - Advantage on inspiration<br>- Advantage on charisma and wisdom | - Disadvantage on dexterity<br>- Disadvantage on intelligence |
